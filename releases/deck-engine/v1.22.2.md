# @deckio/deck-engine v1.22.2

**Released:** 2026-10-27

PDF and PowerPoint capture now preserve the authored slide styling rather than
restyling badges or flattening gradient text.

## What changed

### Fixed

- Preserve badge dimensions, text metrics, editable backgrounds, gradients,
  colours, shadows, SVGs and slide-specific backdrops.
- Wait for fonts and decoded images before fitting slides; handle lazy images,
  failed assets and loading timeouts without hanging or silently dropping content.
- Capture finite animations at their final frame and freeze decorative loops,
  then restore original playback, inline styles, sizing and slide selection.
- Preserve the existing fully-revealed export of progressive-disclosure slides.
- Show actionable, slide-specific failures in the export dialog.

### Changed

- Gate npm publication on engine tests and real Chromium, Firefox and WebKit
  rendering checks, including generated PDF pages and PowerPoint slide images.
